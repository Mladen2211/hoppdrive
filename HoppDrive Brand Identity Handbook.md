# **HoppDrive Brand Identity Handbook**

**Guidelines for HoppDrive \- Kurz- & Langzeitvermietung Schweiz**

## **1\. Introduction**

Welcome to the official Brand Identity Handbook for HoppDrive. These guidelines are designed to ensure consistency across all brand communications. By following these rules, we protect the value of our brand and present a professional, cohesive image to our customers in the Swiss car rental market.

## **2\. Our Logo**

The HoppDrive logo is the primary symbol of our brand. It incorporates three key elements:

1. **The Car Silhouette:** Representing movement, speed, and our core business.  
2. **The Swiss Cross:** Integrated into the letter 'p', symbolizing our quality, reliability, and focus on the Swiss market.  
3. **Cohesive Typography:** A professional, modern typeface that bridges tradition ("hopp") and action ("drive").

### **2.1 Logo Variations**

* **Standard Logo:** Use on white or very light neutral backgrounds.  
* **Monochrome Gray/Black:** For black-and-white printing or when color is not available.  
* **Reversed (White):** For use on the primary red or charcoal backgrounds. Ensure maximum contrast.

### **2.2 Clear Space and Sizing**

* **Clear Space:** Maintain a clear margin around the logo equal to the height of the letter 'h'. Do not place text or other graphics in this zone.  
* **Minimum Size:** The logo should never be reproduced smaller than 35mm (or 100px) wide to ensure legibility.

### **2.3 Logo Misuse**

* **Do NOT** stretch or distort the logo.  
* **Do NOT** change the logo colors outside of the approved variations.  
* **Do NOT** rearrange the elements (car icon, main text, tagline).  
* **Do NOT** place the logo on a busy background that compromises legibility.

## **3\. Typography**

Consistent typography is crucial to our brand voice. Our fonts convey professionalism and modernity.

* **Primary Font (Brand Name):** A clean, modern sans-serif (such as *Fira Sans* or *Open Sans*). Use **Bold** weight for "hoppdrive".  
* **Secondary Font (Tagline & Web Sub-headings):** Use the same sans-serif family in **Regular** or **Light** weight.

## **5\. Tone of Voice**

How we speak is as important as how we look. When writing for HoppDrive, embody these traits:

* **Professional & Authoritative:** We know the Swiss car rental market inside and out. We are competent and reliable.  
* **Efficient & Forward-Looking:** Our language is concise and action-oriented. We help customers get where they are going without hassle.  
* **Trustworthy & Direct:** Avoid jargon. Be honest, clear, and easy to understand. We are dependable partners.

## **6\. Correct Tagline Usage**

The tagline must always appear exactly as:

**Kurz- & Langzeitvermietung Schweiz**

* **Positioning:** It should be placed centrally below the main "hoppdrive" text.  
* **Color:** Set in the **Soft Graphite (\#666666)** color or **Deep Charcoal (\#3E4144)**.  
* **Weight:** The font weight should be significantly lighter (Regular/Light) than the bold main brand name to establish a clear visual hierarchy.
